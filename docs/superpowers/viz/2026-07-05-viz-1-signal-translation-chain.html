“Why not just have ServoDriver move the servo?”

Because a number isn’t a pulse. Six languages sit between 47° and a turning shaft.

ServoDriver is your app’s “print this pose” — it owns the degrees, the clamp, and the current position, then hands off exactly one number. ServoKit is the printer driver for the PCA9685: it owns the microseconds, the duty cycle, the 12-bit ticks, and the I²C bytes. You keep the degrees; the library keeps the electrons.
